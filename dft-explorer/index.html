05 / ADMITTED GRID & DECISIONS

Compare phases only on a compatible reference layer.

Carbon has paired, scoped harmonic-QHA labels at four pressures and four temperatures. The plotted sign is fixed as ΔG = Ggraphite − Gdiamond. Negative values favor graphite.

Only recorded grid points are scored. Sparse grid cells are not a continuous phase boundary. Numerical bounds are scoped to recorded q-mesh and volume-fit sensitivities; they are not total physical uncertainty.

07 / REPRODUCIBILITY

Every visual starts from a recorded file.

What is included

  • All records in the available project compute ledger.
  • Every distinct parseable VASP XML in the two audited archive roots, with duplicate file copies identified.
  • Selected complete Ca NVT archive segments, including the merged 1,600-frame record. Overlapping segments are not independent repeats.
  • Locally paired phonon geometries, force constants and mesh spectra.
  • Versioned carbon and Ti harmonic thermodynamic tables, plus project admission states.

What remains distinct

  • A scheduler exit code, an SCF result and a free-energy admission are different checks.
  • Γ-point eigenmodes do not constitute a full dispersion path.
  • Harmonic/QHA labels do not include general anharmonic, electronic or magnetic entropy.
  • Missing density, dedicated band and remote files stay visible as missing.
  • No DFT, MD or model training was submitted for this page.
