## Interpretation

- A scoped G label does not certify a matched structure–Hessian–entropy probe pair.
- Fixed-volume Fvib, harmonic Svib, Cv, and the fixed-pressure derivative −dG/dT are different labels.
- Source effective-harmonic force constants are not automatically a static equilibrium Hessian.
- Imaginary-mode flags belong to a phase, reference geometry, Hamiltonian and numerical setting. A historical flag does not invalidate a different accepted layer.
- Rows split reference tiers and pressure domains. Repeated tiers are not additional phases or independent materials.
- No label is generated by the evaluated DPA in this reference inventory. Any future DPA-derived Hessian must be added as a model-derived input, not an independent reference.
- Reference provenance is separate from MLIP pretraining overlap; pretraining-data independence has not been established for every label.
- Local evidence only: no live Expanse queue or unknown remote result was inspected for this inventory.
