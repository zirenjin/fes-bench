# Phase 5 delivery — initial external baseline and E3 fixture

## Implemented

- `fes_bench/baselines/bartel2018.py` implements the specified analytical
  temperature correction and documents that fixed-composition polymorphs are
  distinguished only by volume.
- `fes_bench/synthetic/e3.py` deterministically creates four SiO2-scale
  `a + bT + cT ln(T)` curves with 1 meV noise.
- `fes_bench/baselines/interp_const.py` supplies per-phase linear
  interpolation with constant extrapolation; `phase_id_mlp.py` supplies the
  specified two-hidden-layer `[T, phase one-hot]` comparator.
- `fes_bench/synthetic/e3_recovery.py` is a configuration-driven E3 recovery
  command.  It fits the prescribed `a + bT + cT ln(T)` curves for
  `qh_residual` and uses the explicitly different phase-ID MLP basis for
  `repr_only` with `F_QH=0`; it records both basis choices and checks the two
  MAEs are within a factor of two.
- `tests/test_bartel2018.py`, `tests/test_interp_const.py`,
  `tests/test_phase_id_mlp.py`, and `tests/test_synthetic_e3.py` cover these
  building blocks.
- The Bartel formula was checked on 2026-09-19 against Eq. 4 of Bartel *et
  al.*, Nat. Commun. 9, 4168 (2018), DOI
  `10.1038/s41467-018-06682-4`; its units and the fact that volume is the
  only structural discriminator at fixed composition match the implementation.

## Deviations from design

- The published formula is now cross-checked, but a local copy of the original
  supplementary PDF is intentionally not vendored; the DOI is recorded in the
  module docstring instead.
- E3 remains a scalar synthetic recovery harness, not a new DPA training
  result: no atomic descriptor is defined for its intentionally structure-free
  curves.  The `repr_only` MLP and `qh_residual` continuous-tlog paths are
  intentionally distinct, so the reported ratio is a real cross-basis check.
  The synthetic curves are generated by `a + bT + cT ln(T)`; the lower error
  of the tlog basis is therefore a construction result, not evidence for the
  physical method.  This fixture is used only to exclude an implementation
  bug.
- E1/E2 checkpoint reevaluation is still pending.  No production model
  training was started.  The read-only provenance and availability audit is
  recorded in `results/phase5/e1_e2_reanalysis_hold.md`: existing SiO2 models
  target a now-excluded four-phase domain, while the available Hf artifact is
  a results JSON rather than a frozen checkpoint.
