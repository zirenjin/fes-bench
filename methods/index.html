Temperature response

For φ(T) = [1,x,x ln x,x²], x=T/Tₛ:
S(T) = −∂G/∂T = −[c₁+c₂(ln x+1)+2c₃x]/Tₛ.
ΔĜᵢⱼ(T) = φ(T)ᵀ(ĉᵢ−ĉⱼ).

The derivative gives effective entropy for the declared G reference. It does not separate vibrational, electronic, magnetic or other contributions. At fixed volume use −∂F/∂T. A three-source centered structural design has rank at most two, regardless of embedding width or concatenated volume.
