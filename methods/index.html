Scope, status and source

Remove only the appropriate translational zero-mode treatment; do not delete imaginary branches to claim a valid conventional harmonic free energy. Here Φ(q) is the Fourier-transformed force-constant matrix, M the atomic-mass matrix, Nprim the number of atoms in the primitive cell, and q weights sum to one.
