Confirmed findings in the legacy generator and acceptance metadata

This is a first-pass inspection of preserved code and source files. Small synthetic fixtures reproduce the archived control flow. They do not recompute a phonon spectrum or establish the size of every affected model error.

Unstable volumes are not rejected.

The legacy generator computes thermal properties before counting negative frequencies. It returns the thermal table and includes all volumes in the energy minimum. Its negative-frequency threshold is a diagnostic field, not an acceptance gate.

The quartz flag contradicts the selected spectrum.

The materialization config manually supplies qh_reliable=true. All 1,649 raw quartz rows select V/V₀=1 and record a minimum frequency of −1.054 THz. The flag does not certify harmonic stability.

In the same SiO₂ raw exports, selected cristobalite and P6₃/mmc tridymite settings have minimum frequencies of −0.193 and −0.354 THz. Their reliability flags already say false. The raw phase F_QH values match the current canonical exports at the paired temperatures.

Phonopy can produce finite thermal values while excluding nonpositive frequencies from its oscillator sum. The precise mode treatment in the historical run still requires its saved runtime or mode-resolved cache. A finite result is not evidence that imaginary modes were physically resolved. Phonopy cutoff convention.

Historical defects belong to specific branches.

  • The old C2221 fixed-cell export omitted the atom-count division. A correction exists; that branch was excluded from the three active-phase scores.
  • The early Ti polynomial minimizer could miss a lower endpoint when an interior local minimum existed. A fix exists; an earlier check found no changed values in its actual 155-row table under that correction.
  • The legacy Hf curve uses a three-volume discrete minimum at the upper boundary. It is a diagnostic approximation, not a converged pressure-minimized QHA.

These findings do not automatically invalidate the separately reconstructed carbon DFT-QHA layer or the later Ti hcp comparison layer. They use different calculations and acceptance records.

What this changes

The existing QH-versus-direct comparison remains a numerical result for the declared historical inputs. It cannot establish whether verified vibrational physics improves transfer. First map the scored curves to their generator and cache, reconstruct the oscillator sum independently, validate units and mode handling, and check volume minimization. Then rerun the same-function control.

Do not remove genuine unstable modes, take absolute frequencies, or flip the phase subtraction to obtain a favorable baseline. If the validated reference branch is unstable, ordinary QHA is inapplicable there.

No new DFT calculation or residual-model retraining was performed for this audit.
